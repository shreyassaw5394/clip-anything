Clip Anything desktop build - third-party software

Clip Anything itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). Python packages inside this
folder are listed with their licences in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.

ffmpeg and ffprobe (folder "ffmpeg") are separate programs that Clip Anything
starts as child processes; they are not linked into it. They are prebuilt
binaries, used unmodified, licensed under the GNU GPL v2 or later (the builds
enable libx264). The full licence text is in ffmpeg/LICENSE.txt. Source code and
build scripts for the exact builds:
  Windows: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (autobuild-2026-11-12-15-40, win64-gpl-shared)
  macOS:   https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static (b6.1.1; sources via https://ffmpeg.org)
  ffmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
Replacing the files in ffmpeg/ with any ffmpeg >= 5.0 build of your own works.

Fonts, tcl/tk and the Python runtime are redistributed under their own
permissive licences (PSF, Tcl/Tk BSD-style).

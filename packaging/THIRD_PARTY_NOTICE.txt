Clip Anything desktop build - third-party software

Clip Anything itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). Python packages inside this
folder are listed with their licences in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.

ffmpeg and ffprobe (folder "ffmpeg") are separate programs that Clip Anything
starts as child processes; they are not linked into it. They are prebuilt
binaries, used unmodified, configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3
(libx264, libx265 and other GPL components), so the GNU GPL version 3 applies
to them. Licence texts in the ffmpeg folder: LICENSE.txt (the build's own
notice), COPYING.GPLv3.txt, COPYING.GPLv2.txt, COPYING.LGPLv2.1.txt,
COPYING.LGPLv3.txt and FFMPEG-LICENSE.md. Run `ffmpeg -version` and
`ffmpeg -L` in that folder for the build's full configuration and its own
licence statement.

Exact builds (URLs and SHA-256 pinned in packaging/fetch_ffmpeg.py):
  Windows: BtbN FFmpeg-Builds, release autobuild-2026-11-12-15-40,
           ffmpeg-n9.0.2-57-gd838f9f9ae-win64-gpl-shared-9.0.zip
           https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (build scripts and sources)
  macOS:   eugeneware/ffmpeg-static b6.1.1 (ffmpeg 6.0)
           arm64:  https://osxexperts.net   x86_64: https://evermeet.cx/ffmpeg/
           https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static
  ffmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
Replacing the files in ffmpeg/ with any ffmpeg >= 5.0 build of your own works.

## B. More signals (game-agnostic; every one measured before it ships)
1. **Laughter / shout** (audio, mic or mix): a cheap detector (energy + spectral centroid + rhythmic burst pattern for laughter; sustained high-energy voiced for shouts) → signal `voice_react` 0..1; weight into "Funny" and as a small bonus by default.
2. **Big text flash** (video proxy): a large high-contrast region with text-like horizontal structure appearing suddenly in the centre third and holding ≥ 0.5 s → `text_flash`; bonus near a peak (victory/eliminated/level-up/finish), penalty when it is the only thing happening and the screen is otherwise static (menus).
3. **Fade to black / respawn** → `fade`: whole-frame luminance dropping to near-black then recovering; used (a) as a clean clip END snap, (b) as a mild "something ended" bonus just before it.
4. **Streak merge**: peaks within 25 s of each other merge into one clip up to `--max` (default 45; streaks may extend to 60 with a note) — "several bursts become one clip".
5. **Slow-mo / kill-cam** → `slowmo`: runs of near-duplicate proxy frames while audio continues (frame-dup ratio) → bonus.
6. **Music stinger** → `stinger`: strong tonal onset (harmonic peak jump) without broadband noise — a mild bonus only, since cutscene music triggers it too.
7. **Presets and chips** (no AI): `--preset shooter|racing|sports|horror|story|funny` and app chips "Fights · Funny · Big plays · Crashes · Calm but tense" mapping to weight sets + bar adjustments in config (TOML); "Calm but tense" = long low-level build-up followed by a burst (use the existing build-up logic with a longer pre-roll and lower bar on onset).

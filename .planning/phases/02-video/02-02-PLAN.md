## Fixes (game-agnostic only; never per-game HUD rules)
1. **Agreement term**: a real action moment has audio AND motion rising together. Score = w1·weighted mean + w2·max + w3·(audio_z⁺ · motion_z⁺)^0.5 with w3 large; a lone saturated signal cannot top the list on its own. Keep the informativeness gate so quiet games still work (when a signal is globally uninformative, the agreement term degrades to the remaining signal).
2. **Motion saturation**: normalise motion with a rolling z-score and a soft clamp (tanh) so text/overlay flashes and scene cuts don't hit 1.00; cuts are their own (small) bonus, not motion.
3. **Static-card penalty**: windows whose proxy frames are near-identical except for small text-like regions (high fraction of pixels with zero diff + diff confined to thin high-contrast strokes) get a penalty; lobbies, scoreboards, loading screens, menus. Test on the three COD duds; must not fire on real aiming-down-sights moments.
4. **Edges**: de-weight the first and last 30 s of a file (intro/outro) unless agreement is strong; never pick a window that starts at 0:00 unless its score beats the median of the top-N by 2×.
5. **Score floor**: `--count` is a MAX. If fewer windows clear the floor (e.g. ≥ 0.35 after fixes), return fewer clips and say so; never fill with duds.
6. **Snap to cuts** (untested in 02-01): verify on real footage.
